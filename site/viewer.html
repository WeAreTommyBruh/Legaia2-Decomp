Models are textured from the matching .tms file and posed with the clips of the matching .mov file. Untick Pose with animation to see a model at rest. Motion files show on their model, or as a bare skeleton when none is found. Effects are an approximate preview: particle emitters and rings are drawn from their documented fields as plain glows and outlines, without angles, texture animation or the game's exact random spread, and the script underneath is exact. Saved OBJ files are in the unposed bind pose. Everything is decoded from your disc with the same formats as the tools in tools/.





  
Extract assets

  
Writes files into a folder you choose. Nothing leaves your computer. The disc holds about 2.4 GB, so allow some time and disk space.
